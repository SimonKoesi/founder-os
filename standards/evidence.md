# Evidence and research

Label each claim as observed, sourced, estimated or simulated. Record source/date,
scope, units, currency, region, sample size and uncertainty where relevant.
Current market prices, competitors, APIs, legal rules and recommendations require
current authoritative sources; offline tasks explicitly remain unverified.
This migration intentionally ignores external links in the supplied UI index.

Simulated buyer results generate hypotheses. They are neither conversion forecasts
nor calibrated upper bounds, and never customer testimonials. Real experiments
outrank simulations. Report exclusions, changed seeds/pitches and small samples.
Never silently use synthetic demand to validate a business.
Financial outputs are projections conditional on costs, capacity and ramp; separate
operating profit, startup funding, recurring cash and tax. The legacy calculator
uses currency-neutral amounts displayed with `$`; state the actual currency in
the input notes and don't mix currencies. Its Profitable verdict means only that
available model checks pass, not verified market viability or funding advice.
No invented scarcity, endorsements, costs, reviews, scientific trends or legal clearance.
